# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- The toy menu has a gear button in the corner that opens the addon options.

## 1.2.0

- A live cooldown window now appears when you use the Arcantina key, Garrison or Dalaran hearthstone while it's on cooldown. It counts down and closes by itself.
- The "no teleport cloaks" message now points you to the guild vendor.

## 1.1.0

- Six new button icons: Fel dice, Fel swirl, Shadow dice, Shadow swirl, Ember compass and Ember flame.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- One button that uses a random hearthstone toy from the toys you choose, never the same toy twice in a row.
- Toy menu with pool checkboxes, click-to-use, favourites (picked more often) and use counts.
- "See unavailable" view for the hearthstone toys you don't own yet, with a Wowhead link on Shift+click.
- Travel shortcuts for the Arcantina key, Garrison and Dalaran hearthstones.
- Teleport cloak bar with automatic or manual restore of your normal cloak.
- Cooldown swirl, charge display, ready pulse, and a fade-when-idle option.
- Four button icons to choose from.
